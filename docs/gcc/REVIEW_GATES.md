# LAVKOO GCC: review gates

As of 2026-11-18. The register of the reviews that a change needs before it merges or ships, who reads it, what they read first, and where the outcome is written. It answers master tracker requirement M1-13. **No review in this register has been requested or done.** An empty "Outcome" is not a pass; a gate is open until a person has written an outcome here and in the pull request. Nothing in this document merges, approves or deploys anything.

## The rule

A pull request that changes seller money access, privileged administrator authentication, or the Arabic text that people read does not merge and does not ship before the named reviewer has read it and the outcome is written (1) in the pull request's conversation by the reviewer, and (2) in the row of this register, with a date. A review of one head says nothing about the next: when the head moves after a review, the row says which head was read. The reviewer is a person; Claude sessions and CI do not fill an outcome.

What stops a merge mechanically is not this document. It is the rule for `main` in `docs/gcc/BRANCH_PROTECTION.md` (in draft [PR 91](https://github.com/fullstackfull/lavkoo/pull/91): one approving review, code owners, approval of the last push, no force push) and its `CODEOWNERS`, which a person applies. Until they are applied, `main` is unprotected and this register is the only record of what was read (deferred risk DR-4 in [DEFERRED_RISKS.md](DEFERRED_RISKS.md)).

## Review packs

One short file for each review, with the pull requests and their heads, what changes for money or security, the evidence and its CI state, the questions the reviewer decides and an empty outcome table: [R1](reviews/R1-finance-seller-money-access.md), [R2](reviews/R2-security-gcc20-server.md), [R3](reviews/R3-security-gcc20-seller-app.md), [R4](reviews/R4-security-hotfix.md), [R5](reviews/R5-native-arabic-reader.md), [R6](reviews/R6-legal-finance-gcc-money.md). A pack is a reading guide, never an approval: its outcome table is filled only by the reviewer.

## Who can be asked

**Nobody is named, so no review has been requested.** The repository `fullstackfull/lavkoo` has one collaborator, its owner (read from the collaborators list on 2026-11-18); the apps' repositories were not checked for others. No document in the repositories names a finance owner, a security reviewer, a native Arabic reader or a lawyer of a country. A review is requested from a person who holds the authority and whose identity is known, so the owner is asked to name one person for each role (finance, security, native Arabic reader, and a lawyer for each of the five countries); the pack is then sent to them from the pull request and the "Requested" cell is filled with the date. Until then development goes on in the other axes and every gate stays open.

## What the tracker asks for

Each requirement of the [master tracker](MASTER_IMPLEMENTATION_TRACKER.md) names the reviews it needs. Counted over its 359 requirements (a requirement can need more than one): 204 need an owner decision, 117 a security review, 83 a finance review, 45 a legal review and 37 a native Arabic reader; 65 need none. The card of a requirement shows which. A requirement whose review is not done cannot reach READY FOR OWNER REVIEW, whatever its tests say.

## How to record an outcome

In this table, replace "none recorded" with the outcome (approved, approved with changes, refused), the name of the reviewer, the date and **the head that was read** (the eight characters of the commit), and link the review in the pull request. A refusal is recorded the same way and keeps the row's pull requests blocked. Do not edit the other rows. A reviewer who wants a check run again asks for it in the pull request; the evidence of a run is in the audit reports, not here.

## Known gaps

- No review is recorded as done anywhere; the owner guide and the charter name the reviews but hold no outcome.
- The Arabic text of the apps is not in the generated list: it is in files of the app repositories and has to be read there.
- The list of Arabic lines is generated from the pull request refs and is current only on the day it is generated; regenerate it with `docs/gcc/tools/list_arabic_strings.py` after a pull request moves.
- The security review of GCC-20 (R2, R3) has not been requested, and the GCC-20 pull requests state that they are not release ready before it.
