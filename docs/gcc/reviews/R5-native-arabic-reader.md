# R5: Native Arabic reader

As of 2026-11-18. **Not requested. No reader is named. No outcome is recorded.** The register is [REVIEW_GATES.md](../REVIEW_GATES.md). A machine reading is not enough for a legal or money sentence.

## What the reader decides

Whether each Arabic line that a customer, a seller or an administrator reads is correct, natural and says what the English source says. Known case: the master tracker asks for a ruling on المسح versus المسح الضوئي in the seller app's scan strings (K-13).

## Why it matters for money and law

Several lines are legal or money sentences: the wallet is closed in a storefront's currency, a storefront is not sellable yet, the coupon's currency, the refund and the terms. A wrong word there is a promise or a refusal the platform did not mean.
