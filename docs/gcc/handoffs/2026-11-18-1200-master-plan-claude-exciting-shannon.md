2. **Pinned in #98, not changed:** in a cart of two lines each rate row of `order_taxes` keeps half of its line's tax (the divisor adds the rates of every line of the seller's cart). The order and its lines are right; a report that adds the rows up under-reports. Correcting it changes what a report shows: the owner's call.
3. **Found while building #99:** the module's admin screen of the system tax setup read "the" setup as the first row of the table, so a row owned by a storefront with the lowest id would have been shown and edited as the platform's. #99 scopes the three queries.
